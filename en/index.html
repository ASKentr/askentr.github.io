Projects

Problem → solution → result

Izvestia media holdingsince 2024

AI fluency program for a media holding

Problem
Employees had no clear idea how to use AI in news production or what content they could create with it.
Solution
A modular program for different audiences: foundations, advanced prompting and data work, media production (images, video, voice), and a separate module for managers on risk, legal issues and adoption. Every new module is reviewed by a pilot group of managers before launch.
Result
More than 400 newsroom employees and managers trained; each next module is built on participants' requests.

Automating the training cycle

Problem
Sign-ups, attendance, sending materials and reporting took a lot of manual work.
Solution
Local tools built with an AI assistant: attendance tracking, follow-up e-mails with materials, a dashboard for management. A spec for a training sign-up chatbot.
Result
Several times less manual work; personal data never leaves the work computer.

Densito AI

Problem
A task from the Moscow Department of Health: an AI service that checks the quality of bone densitometry scans.
Solution
A two-person team: scan classification with a neural network (CNN), web interface, Docker, CI and automated tests.
Result
A working prototype in a public repository.

AI agentsknowledge

A knowledge base maintained by AI

Problem
Program materials, research and notes were scattered across files and quickly went out of date.
Solution
An Obsidian vault curated by an AI agent: it processes incoming materials, files them by topic, keeps an index and a changelog. Custom skills and MCP connections.
Result
I prepare presentations and course materials several times faster.
AI agentsmedia

Telegram channel monitoring

Problem
Following dozens of channels without missing important news.
Solution
An AI agent goes through the channels, scores posts against set criteria and builds a digest.
Result
A short selection of what matters instead of a feed of hundreds of messages.
